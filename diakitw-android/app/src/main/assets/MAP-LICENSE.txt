Bundled derivative: ISO_A2_EH + all polygon rings rounded to 0.001 degrees. 177 generalized features; small countries/territories may be absent. De facto/generalized boundaries do not establish sovereignty or precise borders. No warranties of accuracy.
Location markers, when shown, come from separately validated IPwho.is coordinates, not Natural Earth country centers. Country-only results have no marker. No GPS, web map tiles, keys or external map requests.
